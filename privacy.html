Privacy policy: conf-tMtTracker

Effective 23 November 2026.

conf-tMtTracker (iOS app and web site) does not collect, store or share personal data on any server operated by its developer. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Data on your device

Your submission plan, research profile, in-app activity used for suggestions (which you can switch off and clear), publications, rule files and Scimago file are stored only on your device. They leave it only when you export a backup file yourself.

Network requests

  • Published conference data and topic profiles are downloaded from tran-minh-thai.github.io (GitHub Pages). These requests contain no personal data.
  • When you look up a publication, the DOI or title you type, or the DOI or title read from a photo or PDF, is sent to the public Crossref API (api.crossref.org), which is governed by Crossref's own privacy policy.

Camera, photos and PDF files (iOS app)

To add a publication from a paper, you can take a picture with the camera, choose one image from your photo library, or choose the paper's PDF file. The text is read on your device. The image or file is not sent anywhere and is not kept; only the DOI or title read from it is looked up as described above. The app sees only the one image or file you choose, not your photo library or your other files.

Notifications

Deadline reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's public repository, https://github.com/tran-minh-thai/conf-tracker.
